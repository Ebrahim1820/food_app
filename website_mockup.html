How it works

Three steps to rescue your next meal

From browsing to pickup — it takes less than 2 minutes to secure a great deal.

🔍

Browse nearby deals

Discover surplus food from restaurants, bakeries, cafés, and supermarkets close to you — filtered by category and distance.

📦

Reserve & pay online

Pick an offer that excites you, choose your quantity, and complete a secure checkout in seconds. No surprises at the door.

🏪

Pick up & enjoy

Head to the store during the pickup window with your order confirmation. Collect your bag, and dig in — you've saved money and food.
